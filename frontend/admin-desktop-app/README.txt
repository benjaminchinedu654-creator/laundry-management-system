=====================================================
LAUNDRY EXPRESS - ADMIN DESKTOP APPLICATION (.NET 8 WPF)
=====================================================

Features Included:
1. Operations Dashboard:
   - Live revenue totals (All time & Today)
   - Order volume metrics & new orders count
   - Active in-progress orders (pickup, washing, ready)
   - Delivered orders & registered customer counts
   - Real-time orders breakdown by stage
   - Recent orders feed with customer contacts

2. Orders Management:
   - Filter by stage (Pending, Picked Up, Washing, Ready, Out for Delivery, Delivered, Cancelled)
   - Filter by payment status (Unpaid, Paid, Refunded)
   - Instant search by Order Code, Customer Name, or Phone
   - Deep Order Inspector:
     * Full pickup address, date & time slot
     * Delivery address, date & time slot
     * Items breakdown with category, quantities & line totals
     * Financial totals (Subtotal & Total)
     * Customer phone, email & special notes
   - One-click Stage Advancer (Pending -> Picked Up -> Washing -> Ready -> Out For Delivery -> Delivered -> Cancelled)
   - Direct Payment Status Updater

3. Services & Pricing Catalog:
   - Category filter (Wash & Iron, Dry Clean, Iron Only, Specialty)
   - Service item details & live rates in Naira (₦)
   - Add new laundry service
   - Edit existing service pricing and description
   - 1-click Toggle Availability (Active / Disabled)
   - Delete service with safety confirmation

4. Customer Directory:
   - Registered customer accounts list
   - Phone, email, delivery addresses
   - Lifetime order counts & total spent
   - Instant toggle between Active and Suspended status

5. Payments Ledger:
   - Transaction list (Cash, Card, Transfer)
   - Payment statuses (Success, Pending, Failed, Refunded)
   - Record manual cash/transfer payments with order reference

6. Financial & Revenue Reports:
   - Settled revenue summary
   - Daily collected revenue breakdown log

Quick Launch:
- Double-click "run-fast.bat" to start in fast development mode.
- Double-click "LaundryAdminApp.exe" for the compiled standalone binary.
